What the finance check needs to return

The panel is driven by the reason and the amounts. Everything shown here is placeholder data.

  reason: "DEPOSIT_TOO_LOW" | "BUDGET_TOO_LOW"
        | "BOTH" | "BELOW_MIN_BORROW"
        | "VEHICLE_CRITERIA" | "UNKNOWN",
  currentDeposit: 750,
  currentMonthlyBudget: 150,
  minBorrow: 4000,
  fixes: [{
    lever: "deposit" | "budget",
    shortfall: 250,

  }]
}
  • No amounts returned: show the reason copy only, without the fix cards.
  • UNKNOWN or VEHICLE_CRITERIA: keep today's generic panel and the Account Manager actions.
  • Monthly figures are estimates until the in-place re-check returns a real quote.

Copy and compliance

  • No promise of a quote before the re-check. Copy says "you could get a quote".
  • Newer and lower-mileage cars are framed as "lenders are often more flexible", not "cheaper". Compliance should sign off any wording that implies a saving.
  • Styling follows the live Members' Area (Inter, lavender panel, green for the main fix only) rather than the Helvetica Neue print guidelines.
